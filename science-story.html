What It Is

By the time we get to Science Story™, the research is done and the claims are written. Now the creative work starts. A Science Story™ is a campaign idea built from your science: a headline, a story told in your brand’s voice, and the claims that hold it up.

It isn’t a script, and it isn’t an ad that goes live. It’s the story your brand should tell with its science, the idea your ads, product pages, creators and sales teams all take their cue from.

Where research meets creative

This is where the science starts to sound like your brand. Less lab, more campaign. Everything still rests on the claims, so it stays approvable.

A campaign you can picture

Read one and you can see the commercial, the product page and the creator’s post. It gives every team and agency the same idea to build from.

Tested before you spend

Real consumers tell you which story, which headline and which claims move them. You launch an idea that’s been proven, not one somebody guessed at.

Here’s what the bottle never mentions. The milligrams on the label tell you how much magnesium is in the capsule. They don’t tell you how much of it your body takes in. And not every form of magnesium is absorbed the same way.

Most magnesium on the shelf is magnesium oxide. Lullwell is built on magnesium glycinate, a form that in studies was absorbed 2.7 times better than oxide at the same dose. More of what you take, actually put to work, supporting relaxed muscles and a calm wind-down.*

Consumer Testing

Three concepts go in. Consumers pick one.

We put all three concepts in front of real shoppers through our research partner, Persuadable Research. Each story is judged on its own first, then claim by claim, then against the others.

Step 1The right people~100 per story

A national online sample, screened to the shopper you sell to. Each person sees one concept only, so nothing is judged against the others yet.

Step 2The story aloneHeadline + Science Story

No claims yet. Purchase intent, how new and different it feels, how believable it is, and how well the headline grabs attention. Then they highlight the words they like and the words they don’t.

Step 3Every claim, one by oneClaim by claim

Each claim is rated on two things: do they believe it, and would it change whether they buy. That is how the lead claim gets picked.

Step 4Head to headAll three stories

Everything together, side by side. Which one would most get them to buy, and why, read across age, gender and the brands they use today.

You also get the line-by-line heat map, every claim ranked by believability and purchase impact, the groups each story wins with, and a recommendation: which story, which headline, and which claim to lead with.
