What It Is

Science Story™ is our proprietary framework for turning clinical data, real-world evidence, and category knowledge into a narrative that persuades — unlocking new positioning and new reasons for consumers to believe. The product of more than a decade in consumer health.

Instead of asking:

"What can we say?"

We ask:

"What have we not discovered yet?"

We start from the real constraints

Not a fantasy playing field. The regulatory class your product sits in shapes the story from the first draft, which is why these narratives survive review.

Our team first, AI second

A structured curiosity framework, run by people who have done this in consumer health for over a decade. AI accelerates inside that framework rather than replacing it.

Scored like investments

Directions are judged on defensibility and strategic value, not on which one reads prettiest. One Science Story™ then powers every channel you sell in.

How It Works

From verified claims to a story consumers chose.

Swipe through the four stages of a Science Story™.

01 · Claim Clusters

Claims that argue together, not a list.

The substantiated claims from Science Intelligence™ get grouped into clusters, each one factual, specific and pointed at a single idea. A cluster is what gives a story something to stand on.

One cluster

CL-01
The label tells you how much is in it. Absorption tells you how much you get.
CL-12
Absorption has been studied for forty years. This formula is built on what those studies found.
CL-02
It only works where it is absorbed. This is built for how it absorbs.
02 · The narrative

A throughline a brand can own.

We present three to five coherent directions, not a buffet of loose facts. Each is one narrative with the claims that hold it up attached to it, so you are choosing between finished arguments rather than imagining your way from a claim list to a campaign.

Direction 01

Same milligrams. Not the same magnesium.

The number on the label is how much is in the capsule. It says nothing about how much crosses into your blood, and across published trials the gap between forms runs several-fold.

Consumer Testing

We test the story before you spend on it.

Healthcare brands expect ideas to be validated. So we build two to three complete directions and put them in front of real consumers, head to head, before a dollar goes to production. You back the concept consumers chose, not the one that tested best in the room.

Scored whole, then in layers

Purchase intent, uniqueness and believability on the full concept, then separate reads on the headline, the narrative and every individual claim.

A highlighter heat map

Consumers mark the words that pull them in and the words that push them away, so you see line by line what is carrying the concept and what is costing it.

A ranked answer

A max-difference exercise forces trade-offs and ranks every claim by its real power to move a purchase, so you know exactly which message leads.

We can run testing through our partner, Persuadable Research, or package everything cleanly for the testing partner you already trust.

FAQ

Science Story™ — common questions

What is a Science Story™?

Your evidence, turned into ownable positioning and a narrative competitors can't easily copy. It's the full output of the Science Marketing Engine.

What's included in a Science Story™?

A literature review, a claims architecture, and the polished narrative — ownable positioning, audience messaging, and creative direction, ready to activate across channels.

How is a Science Story™ different from a brand story?

A brand story is built from creative instinct. A Science Story™ is built from your science first, so it's both compelling and defensible under scrutiny.

How long does a Science Story™ take?

Weeks, not months — and it becomes the foundation every downstream video, ad, and campaign is built from.

What can I do with a Science Story™?

Activate it everywhere: MOA Moment™ videos, social, HCP education, retail and PDP content, influencer kits, and ad-to-education sequences.
