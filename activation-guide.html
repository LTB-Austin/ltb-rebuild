Before You Go Live

Tag your links.

Add UTM tags to every link so you can see which placements drive results. A UTM tag is a short snippet on the end of a link. The viewer never sees it, but your analytics will know exactly where each click came from, so you can tell TikTok traffic from email traffic at a glance.

  • utm_source — where the click came from. tiktok, youtube, email
  • utm_medium — the type of channel. social, cpc, email
  • utm_campaign — which campaign it belongs to. spring_launch

Before

yourbrand.com/product

After

yourbrand.com/product?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spring_launch

You do not have to build these by hand. Google’s free Campaign URL Builder creates a tagged link for you. Keep names lowercase and consistent so reports stay clean. Ask your Let There Be team if you want a tagging system set up for the campaign.
