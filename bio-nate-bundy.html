About

Nate understood what Let There Be does before he ever walked in the door. A friend already here described the work, he read the website, and it landed. He takes that as saying something about the company rather than about himself, since making a stranger understand the work from the outside is precisely the job it does for clients.

“

A place that can make a stranger understand the work from the outside is a place that knows what it is. That’s the same job we do for clients, pointed at ourselves.

Nate Bundy

He arrived from twenty-five years in youth ministry, which he had entered after starting college as a biology major on the pre-med track, and which he left because his life stage changed rather than because anything went wrong. Two children in college and one still at home meant he wanted hours defined enough to be present, and work that still mattered. For five of those ministry years he also worked as an EMT, which kept the biology side of his brain in use the whole time.

So the human body was never the surprise here. The science inside the products was. He is on Coppertone at the moment, having spent his life assuming SPF was the only number that counted, and he has become a genuine convert to AI as a working tool, turning full meeting transcripts into action plans. The project he would point to is the one furthest from the usual format, the Emerson Group TSE party invite.

The rest of his time goes to the things that did not change when the job did. He is a father first, plays Dungeons & Dragons across several groups, and is still involved in church work, which twenty-five years does not simply switch off.
