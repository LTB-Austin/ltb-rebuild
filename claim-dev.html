Science Intelligence

New things to say about your product, straight from the science.

What It Is

Claims are what your brand gets to say. They’re on the front of the pack, in every ad and on every product page, and they decide how a shopper sees your product before they’ve tried it. Claim Dev™ takes what your Lit Review™ found and turns it into new claims: written in your brand’s voice, for the people you’re talking to, and inside the rules your category plays by.

How We Write A Claim

From the science to what you say.

We take everything the research turned up and pull out what your product can actually use. Then we write it like marketing, because that’s what it is.

01Find what’s ownable

What the science says about your product that nobody on the shelf is saying, and what your formula has that theirs doesn’t.

02Change the positioning

Your efficacy language is often already set by your label or monograph. We bring new language from the science: a new angle, a new reason to choose you.

03Write it for the audience

Branded and marketing-forward. One version for shoppers, one for doctors and pharmacists, each in the voice your brand already uses.

Anatomy Of A Claim

A hero claim, and every way to say it.

Claims arrive in directions. Each one has a hero line, a set of supporting lines that say it other ways, the evidence it rests on, and the compliance logic that keeps it standing. The first two are for your brand team. The last two are for legal.

Hero claim · The form on the label

“Most magnesium on the shelf is oxide. In studies, glycinate was absorbed 2.7× better. Lullwell is glycinate.”

Why it matters: every bottle on the shelf leads with milligrams. The form decides how much of them you absorb, and nobody in the category says so.

Number-forward

“2.7× better absorbed than magnesium oxide, at the same dose.”

Its own sentence. Dose stays attached.
Brand voice

“Read the form, not just the milligrams.”

Rests on the label alone.
Names a competitor

“Brand X is magnesium oxide. Lullwell is glycinate.”

Fact from their label. Legal’s call.
Bolder voice

“Your magnesium might be mostly going to waste.”

Implies an outcome. Brand’s call.
The evidence

A crossover trial at the product’s dose and form, backed by a review of 14 trials. Full text read on both.

Compliance logic

Absorption, never sleep outcomes. “In studies,” never “clinically proven.” The 2.7× is attributed to the form, never to a test of this product.

Illustrative · Lullwell™ is a fictional product

The Claim Ladder

You choose how far up the ladder you go.

Every idea gets written from the quietest version to the boldest. Where to stop is your call: every company draws that line in a different place, and the ladder shows you exactly what each step up asks of you.

Level 1Educational

“Not all magnesium is absorbed the same way. Check the form on your label.”

No product, no comparison.
Level 2Category fact

“7 of 10 leading magnesium supplements use magnesium oxide.”

A fact anyone can check on the shelf.
Level 3Contrast

“Most magnesium on the shelf is oxide. Lullwell is glycinate, 2.7× better absorbed.”

The product enters, in its own sentence.
Level 4Named contrast

“Brand X uses magnesium oxide. Lullwell uses glycinate.”

Names a competitor. Legal’s call.
Level 5Conclusion

“Lullwell works better than Brand X.”

Retired. Absorption is not the same as effect.

Illustrative · Lullwell™ is a fictional product; “Brand X” stands in for a named competitor

FAQ

Common questions

What is Claim Dev™?

Claim Dev™ turns your science into new things your brand can say: hero claims and supporting lines, written for your audience and your category, each tied to the studies behind it.

What does Claim Dev™ deliver?

A graded, searchable claims library: structure-function claims mapped to evidence, source-log verification, and language built to survive medical, legal, and regulatory (MLR) review.

Will the claims survive MLR?

That's the point. Every claim is evidence-graded and traceable to the published record, so your legal and regulatory teams can verify it quickly and approve with confidence.

How long does Claim Dev™ take?

Typically a matter of months. In one engagement, a 35-claim, MLR-approved roadmap was delivered in three weeks — while the product was still being formulated.

Do I need a Lit Review™ first?

Often, yes. Lit Review™ surfaces the evidence; Claim Dev™ organizes it into approvable claims. Together they form the foundation of your Science Story™.

How is this different from claims software?

Software suggests claim language and scores its risk automatically; your team still validates it and carries it through review. We deliver finished claim development: graded, tied to verified sources, scoped to your regulatory class, and supported through MLR. We wrote a fuller comparison here: IngredientAI vs. Let There Be.
