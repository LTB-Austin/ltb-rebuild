What Happens In The Lab

Real brand challenges. Real literature. Real takeaways.

The Lab is a preview of Science Intelligence™, not a full literature review. In thirty minutes we show you what an AI-assisted pass on your product turns up, and where a full engagement would go from there.

Real brand challenges

You submit your actual product and claims question, not a hypothetical.

Real literature

Our researchers run a preview pass before the event, using AI the way we have refined it over years of client work, and show you what came back.

Real takeaways

A preview of the findings and a few candidate claim directions, in hand at the end of the session. Printed copy and same-day email.

Manual literature reviews are slow, expensive, and bottleneck R&D teams. So the review gets deferred, and new claims are never found. At the RSQ Claims Lab, experience first-hand how our team, and the way we have learned to put AI to work on the evidence, accelerates literature reviews and surfaces new claim opportunities faster and at scale. The tools are only half of it. The other half is knowing what to ask, what holds up, and what a regulator will accept. This is a preview rather than a finished review, and it is enough to show you what the science on your product could support.

How It Works

Three simple steps: Submit, Schedule, Session.

01 · Submit

Fill out the form below with your claims challenge and a link to your product.

02 · Schedule

Pick a 30-minute slot on our calendar. Book around the conference programming — networking breaks, meal windows, and before or after sessions are ideal.

03 · Session

Your preview is ready before you arrive. We walk you through what the pass surfaced, a few of the strongest studies, the claim directions they point to, and what a full Science Intelligence™ engagement would cover. You leave with a printed copy, emailed the same day.
