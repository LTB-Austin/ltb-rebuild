What To Expect

Experience the future of claim development.

Thirty minutes, your real product, and everything the published science says about it. Give us a brand and a product when you book. That is all we need. Before you arrive we run an AI-assisted pass across the literature, and in the room we show you what came back: the strongest studies, the claim directions they open, and the ground your category has left unclaimed.

No preparation. Nothing confidential. Come with a question, leave with a printed report your team is free to use.
