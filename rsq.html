What Happens In The Lab

Real brand challenges. Real literature. Real takeaways.

Real brand challenges

You submit your actual product and claims question, not a hypothetical.

Real literature

We run the search before the event and present what we actually found.

Real takeaways

Findings and candidate claims, in hand, at the end of the session. Printed copy and same-day email.

Claims development starts with a literature review. Reviews are slow, expensive, and land on the desks of R&D and regulatory teams already stretched thin. So the review gets deferred, and the claim never gets made. The bottleneck isn’t the evidence. It’s the time it takes to find it and validate it. The Claims Lab compresses that into a 30-minute session on your own product.

How It Works

Four steps. Thirty minutes on site.

01 · Submit

Fill out the form below with your claims challenge and a link to your product.

02 · Schedule

Pick a 30-minute slot on the calendar. Book around the conference programming — networking breaks, meal windows, and before or after sessions are ideal.

03 · Session

Your report is ready before you arrive. We walk you through the literature, the strongest studies, and the claim territories it opens.

04 · Take it with you

Every attendee leaves with a printed copy, emailed the same day. Work you can hand to your team on Monday.
