About

Alexander and Alex have been friends for fifteen years, which is the short version of how he ended up here. The longer one runs through six years of San Francisco SaaS, a stretch in California cannabis building equity programs designed to get legal revenue back to the communities the War on Drugs had taken it from, and a dispensary in upstate New York.

When COVID closed the expansion he had been hired to run, he moved back east, picking central New York because he had gone to college there and wanted to be inland and near fresh water. A post about looking for work found the one person who had known him longest. What convinced him this was more than a job came later, with Science Intelligence™, which took work that had always been good and finally gave it somewhere to go.

“

The work here was always good. What changed is that now I can see where it’s going.

Alexander Fabian

His own contribution to that was the Sizzle Reel, which he came up with and then pushed through production as a sales tool. The difficulty was that he knew exactly what he wanted and none of the words a production team uses for it, so he learned them. He has since become fluent in AI and prompt logic as well, which is not where he expected a sales career to lead.

He does all of it from five acres in central New York, which carry a permanent to-do list of firewood, mowing and pool chemistry, cows in view from the porch, and a very good stereo running hard bop, classical and blues most of the day. The one habit the job has actually changed is a small one: since the Listerine project, he uses mouthwash morning and night.
