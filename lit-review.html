What It Is

A Lit Review™ starts with your product: what’s in it, how much, and in what form. We find what’s been published on each of those ingredients for what your product is meant to do, check it against what competitors already claim, and sort it into what you can use, what’s new, what nobody is saying yet, and what doesn’t hold up.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a Lit Review™?

A Lit Review™ is a fast, strategic review of the published science behind your product — what’s established about your ingredients, what’s new, what nobody is claiming yet, and the claims you could credibly own. It's Phase 1 of the Science Marketing Engine.

How long does a Lit Review™ take?

A few months, not the year-plus a traditional review can take. Our AI-assisted process compresses the timeline without cutting corners on rigor.

Who is a Lit Review™ for?

Consumer health and healthcare brands deciding where to invest, what to claim, or how to differentiate — ideally before committing to a formula or a campaign.

What do I get from a Lit Review™?

A clear map of the evidence: mechanisms, claim opportunities scored against the standard that matters (retailer or regulatory), competitive white space, and a prioritized direction for marketing.

How is it different from a standard literature review?

A standard review tells you what the studies say. A Lit Review™ tells you where your brand can credibly win — built for marketing decisions, not just academic completeness.

How is this different from an AI literature-search tool?

A search tool gives your team a faster way to find papers. It does not screen them, verify that identifiers resolve, read the load-bearing ones in full text, or decide what the evidence will and will not support. That work is the review, and it is what we deliver. We wrote a fuller comparison here: IngredientAI vs. Let There Be.
