About

Lindsey went to Boston University dual-enrolled in film school and ad school, studying screenwriting alongside copywriting. She has a background in comedy writing, stand-up, sketch and television. Before Let There Be she worked at America’s Test Kitchen, assisting television and podcast producers and later reporting freelance for the podcast Proof, where she pitched, researched and recorded a forty-minute historical audio documentary. She was a Peter Roth Fellow at the Paley Center for Media, is a certified personal trainer, and still works as a romance bookseller.

She found her way here by posting her copywriting portfolio on LinkedIn while working on personal writing projects. A connection reposted it, it landed on Alex’s feed, and more than a year later her contract has grown considerably. She is proudest of her recent work on MiraSOFT and Cold-EEZE, both of which show how healthcare content can be engaging while staying clinically accurate and inside MLR. Her first ad with Let There Be was “Plan B: Real Talk”, an explainer on how Plan B actually works, which set out to debunk the common myth that it is an abortive drug.

She describes the job as having taught her a second language, not just MOA and MLR but the science behind antihistamines, hip and joint products, and the essential oils that target plaque. The work on PetIQ has her thinking about proactive care and supporting her body earlier rather than later. Outside the office she is a romance book nerd, goes to the beach, has got seriously into Lagree, cooks a great deal with a prized Dutch oven, makes collages, and is always writing something of her own, whether a pilot, a book or a new stand-up set.
