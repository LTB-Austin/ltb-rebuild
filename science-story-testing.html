Science Story™ — Consumer Testing

Tested before you spend.

Before your brand commits to a campaign, real people read the stories and tell you which one moves them, which headline grabs them and which claims they believe.

Step 1 · Who We Test With

It starts with the right people.

Every project is different. At kickoff we work with your brand team to decide who the test should reach and how to run it, so the people reading your stories are the people you sell to.

Step 2 · Three Concepts

The stories go in once they’re ready.

Testing starts after your team has seen the Science Story™ concepts and given feedback. Three go into the test, each one complete: the headline, the full story and the claims behind it.

Headline

It’s not how much magnesium. It’s how much gets in.

Science Story™

It’s 10:40. You took your magnesium an hour ago, just like the bottle said. You’re still awake, and you’re starting to wonder if it does anything at all.

Here’s what the bottle never mentions. The milligrams on the label tell you how much magnesium is in the capsule. They don’t tell you how much of it your body takes in. And not every form of magnesium is absorbed the same way.

Most magnesium on the shelf is magnesium oxide. Lullwell is built on magnesium glycinate, a form that in studies was absorbed 2.7 times better than oxide at the same dose. More of what you take, actually put to work, supporting relaxed muscles and a calm wind-down.*

Same milligrams. Not the same magnesium. Lullwell™ Magnesium Night.

Science Story™

You’ve been tired since three o’clock. Then you finally lie down, and your mind decides now is the perfect time to replay every conversation you had today.

That’s the part most sleep products miss. A tired body and a busy mind are two different problems, and they need two different answers.

Lullwell pairs magnesium with L-theanine, an amino acid found in tea leaves that has been studied for supporting relaxation. The quiet kind, that doesn’t leave you foggy the next morning. Magnesium helps your muscles let go. L-theanine helps your thoughts follow.*

Your body’s ready for bed. Now your mind can be too. Lullwell™ Magnesium Night.
