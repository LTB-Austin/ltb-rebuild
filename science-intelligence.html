What It Is

Science Intelligence™ is where we take on anything in consumer health that comes down to evidence. Lit Review™ and Claim Dev™ are the core of it. But the same team, the same AI tools and fifteen years inside consumer health can be pointed at almost any problem: a formula, an ingredient, a competitor’s claim, a label, or the way a rating site or an AI assistant judges your product.

If the answer is somewhere in the science, the label or the data, this is where we go and get it.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Science Intelligence?

It’s the research stage of the Science Marketing Engine, and the home for any consumer-health question that comes down to evidence. Lit Review™ and Claim Dev™ are the core. We also take on custom work, from challenging a competitor’s claim to weighing a reformulation.

What does it include?

Lit Review™ (everything published on your ingredients, sorted into what you can use), Claim Dev™ (new things your brand can say) and Science Index™ (your evidence, published so AI assistants can find it). Anything else is scoped as a custom project.

Does it have to be a big project?

No. Some questions take an afternoon with the label and a few papers. A full Lit Review™ and Claim Dev™ take a few months. We scope the work to the question.

Where does Science Intelligence fit?

It's the first stage of the Science Marketing Engine — most brands start here, then carry the proof into Science Story™ and Science Studio. It's the foundation everything else is built on.

Does our science and data stay private?

Yes. All AI-assisted research runs in an enterprise-only environment, audited to bank- and healthcare-grade standards, and your data is contractually excluded from AI model training. Your science stays yours.

What’s your next breakthrough?

Start with Science Intelligence — and discover what you can credibly say, in months rather than years.
