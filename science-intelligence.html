01 · The brief

We start where your claims stop.

What do you already say, what have you been told you cannot say, and what would you say if the evidence let you? That is the brief. Everything downstream is scoped to it.

02 · Lit Review™

Every study that could matter, read and recorded.

Peer-reviewed literature and trial registries, searched directly and screened to human evidence at a relevant dose. Load-bearing studies are read in full text, not from the abstract.

The evidence file

Crossover RCT · 46 adultsPositive Full text

Higher serum levels from the organic salt at an identical elemental dose.

Observational cohort · n = 1,940Null Abstract

No association once diet was controlled for. Recorded against the claim.

03 · Claim Dev™

The evidence becomes language you can use.

Each claim is broken into its component parts, substantiated piece by piece, written for the audience that has to be moved, and graded on evidence strength and regulatory risk.

A claim, graded

“The label tells you how much magnesium is in it. Absorption tells you how much you get.”

Evidence strengthStrong
Approval riskLow
04 · Guardrails

What the evidence will not carry, stated up front.

Every claim ships with a plain note on what it does not support. Reviewers stop guessing, approvals move faster, and nothing quietly stretches into territory the science never covered.

Guardrail

Supports: an absorption claim at this dose and form.

Does not support: sleep, anxiety or cognition claims.

05 · What you receive

A file your reviewers can work from.

The evidence workbook, the graded claim set with guardrails attached, and a method statement recording how many studies were screened and how many were read in full. It goes straight into Science Story™.

Deliverables

  • Evidence workbook — every study, its direction and identifier
  • Claim set — graded for evidence strength and regulatory risk
  • Guardrails — what each claim will not carry
  • Method statement — screened, and read in full

FAQ

Science Intelligence — common questions

What is Science Intelligence?

It's the proof stage of the Science Marketing Engine — AI-powered literature reviews and claim development that establish what your science genuinely supports and what you're allowed to say, before any creative begins.

What does it include?

Lit Review™ (the evidence map) and Claim Dev™ (the pre-compliant claims pulled from that evidence). Claims Lab™ is a separate, ongoing retainer you can add to keep that work running between projects.

Why is it faster and cheaper than the traditional route?

A new clinical study runs $1M+ over one to three years; a manual literature review runs ~$500K over six-plus months. Our AI-assisted process reaches the same depth of evidence in months, for under $100K.

Where does Science Intelligence fit?

It's the first stage of the Science Marketing Engine — most brands start here, then carry the proof into Science Story™ and Science Studio. It's the foundation everything else is built on.

Does our science and data stay private?

Yes. All AI-assisted research runs in an enterprise-only environment, audited to bank- and healthcare-grade standards, and your data is contractually excluded from AI model training. Your science stays yours.
